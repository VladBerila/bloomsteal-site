Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Bloomsteal is a game. It has no accounts, and it never asks for your name, email, contacts, photos or location.

What the game stores

Your progress (stars, flowers, settings) is stored on your device and, if you use iCloud, synced to your own iCloud account so your other devices can continue where you left off. We can't see it.

Anonymous statistics

To balance levels and fix crashes, the game sends anonymous gameplay events (for example "level 12 finished with 2 stars") and crash reports through Google Firebase. These are not linked to your identity and are not used to track you across apps. You can turn them off in Settings → "Share anonymous gameplay statistics".

Advertising

Free players may see ads from Google AdMob between levels, plus optional videos for hints. After the tutorial, the game asks once whether you allow tracking (Apple's App Tracking Transparency). Only if you choose "Allow" does Google AdMob use your device's advertising identifier to show ads that are more relevant to you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are not personalised. You can change this any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Players in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are also shown Google's consent form, which can be reopened from Settings → "Ad privacy choices". Gameplay statistics are never used for tracking. Google may use device-level information such as approximate region to serve ads; see how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Anyone who makes a purchase sees no interstitial ads.

Purchases and Game Center

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. Leaderboards and achievements use Apple Game Center, under Apple's privacy policy.

Children

The game is suitable for everyone and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are limited to general-audience content.
