How do I play?

Drag a seed into the garden and lift to plant. Every flower belongs to the closest seed. Drop a seed right onto a flower to "hug" it: nothing can get closer. From level 4, Bramble answers every seed you plant, so think one move ahead.

I bought something on another device

Open the Shop (bag icon on the home screen) or Settings and tap Restore purchases.

My progress

Progress is saved on your device and synced to your other devices through iCloud when you're signed in.
